Our approach

We treat the person, not just the symptom.

Homoeopathy looks at your health as a whole. Every consultation at Care Homoeo Clinic follows the same careful steps.

  1. 01

    Listening first

    A detailed first consultation about your symptoms, history, lifestyle, sleep, diet and emotional wellbeing. We take time to understand how you feel.

Ask the clinic
How do I book an appointment?

All consultations are by appointment. Call +91 90287 69373 to book a time that suits you.

What happens in the first consultation?

The first visit is a detailed conversation about your main complaint, your medical history, lifestyle, diet, sleep and emotional health. This helps the doctor choose the right remedy for you.

What should I bring?

Please bring any previous prescriptions, recent test reports and a list of the medicines you are currently taking.

Can I continue my current medicines?

Please don't stop any prescribed medicine on your own. Tell the doctor about everything you are taking, and she will advise you on how homoeopathic treatment fits in with it.

Is homoeopathy suitable for children?

Yes. The clinic sees patients of all ages, including infants and children. Remedies and advice are adjusted for each child.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on the condition and on the person. Sudden (acute) complaints often respond sooner. Long-standing (chronic) conditions usually need regular follow-ups over a longer period.
